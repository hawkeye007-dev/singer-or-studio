## What belongs in the paper

Use the frozen encoders and fixed trial design as a robustness and evaluation contribution. Keep ranking error, descriptive EER and gallery top-rank error distinct. State the condition and comparison group with every large effect. For reuse and reproducibility, cite the original papers and data releases. [Checked related work](related_work_checked.md) and [venue references](venue-references.md) help locate the primary sources. The older [literature review](literature_review_production_mismatch.md) is historical search material. Confirm citation details against the primary references before reuse.
